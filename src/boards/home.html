सप्तविंशति नक्षत्राणि

Twenty-seven mansions of the Moon

Before the zodiac reached India, the sky was read by where the Moon rested each night. Each mansion spans 13° 20′ and is marked by a junction star. Choose one, or play a month and watch the Moon walk through all twenty-seven.

त्रिस्कन्धं ज्योतिषम्

From the Moon's path, one thread becomes three.

Varāhamihira names three skandhas, branches, of Jyotisha. Every lesson at the Vedshala begins with the Nakshatras and follows one of them.

ग्रन्थ · grantha

A book, in Sanskrit, is a thing that is tied.

The word comes from granth, to tie: palm leaves strung on a cord through two holes. The Vedshala keeps about 3,000 such manuscripts, on palm leaf and paper, beside some 20,000 printed books. Each one published here carries its scan, a transliteration, a translation and a note.

देवप्रयाग · दर्शन

Two rivers meet below the hill and become the Ganga.

The Alaknanda and the Bhagirathi join at Devprayag, the first of the Panch Prayag. The Vedshala stands above them on Dashrathanchal hill, about three hours by road from Rishikesh.

Devprayag at dusk · photograph Ashwini Chaudhary, Unsplash · a stand-in until the family's own

Come for the dusk and stay for the dark. The family opens the telescope on clear nights, and the darkest nights are around the new Moon.
