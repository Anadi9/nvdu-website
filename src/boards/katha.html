संस्थान · the institution

Shri Laxmidhar Acharya Chakradhar Joshi Vidyamandir Shodh Sansthan

The Vedshala is run by the family's research institute, named for the founder. It is a cluster centre of the Gyan Bharatam Mission, which surveys, conserves and publishes India's manuscripts.

द्वादशाङ्गुल शङ्कु · the instruments

A stick twelve fingers tall tells the hour, the season and the place.

The śaṅku is the oldest instrument in the Siddhāntas, and the Vedshala still keeps one beside the 1930 telescope and the Surya and Dhruv Ghati. This one stands on the hill now; move the Sun through today and read its shadow.
