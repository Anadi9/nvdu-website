सिद्धान्त · instrument one

Turn the Moon away from the Sun.

A tithi is the time the Moon takes to gain 12° on the Sun. Thirty of them make a lunar month: fifteen while the Moon waxes, fifteen while it wanes.

संहिता · instrument two

Five limbs make a day.

The pañcāṅga reads each day by five measures of the Sun and Moon, taken at sunrise. Step through the days and watch them change.

होरा · instrument three

Draw a kuṇḍalī for any moment.

The houses stay fixed; the signs turn through them. The first house holds the lagna, the sign rising in the east at that moment over Devprayag. Change the time and watch the sky wheel.
