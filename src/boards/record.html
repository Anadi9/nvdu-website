टिप्पणी · a note on this leaf

This is one loose leaf from a bundle on the shelves. Its number, ४६४२, is written in the top corner, the way every leaf in the is found again.

The writing runs edge to edge with no spaces between words, and each phrase closes with a . Water has soaked in from the right and a lower corner has worn away, which is why it is among the first bundles in the survey.
