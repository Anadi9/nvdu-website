दर्शन · visit

Getting there, and what to bring.

Dashrathanchal hill, Devprayag, Tehri Garhwal. About three hours by road from Rishikesh on the Badrinath highway.

पहचान · at the gate

Look for the painted sign.

Red and blue on white, rusted at the edges: नक्षत्र-वेधशाला, Observatory, स्थापना 1946, Research Library. Along the bottom, its founder: आचार्य पं. चक्रधर जोशी.

{{f.k}}
{{f.v}}

BRING

  • A warm layer: the hill is cold after dark, even in autumn
  • A torch with a red light, to keep your eyes used to the dark
  • Shoes for steps and a slope up the hill
  • Patience: eyes take about twenty minutes to see faint stars
